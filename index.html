7-Day Aim Training

Build skill through consistent practice.

1

Warm Up

Spend a few minutes practicing basic movement and crosshair control.

2

Crosshair Practice

Keep your crosshair around likely enemy positions.

3

Drag Practice

Practice controlled upward movement in the training area.

4

Movement

Practice strafing, crouching and changing direction smoothly.

5

Short Matches

Apply your settings in normal matches and observe what feels comfortable.

6

Review

Identify mistakes and adjust only one setting at a time.

7

Repeat

Repeat the routine consistently instead of changing settings constantly.
